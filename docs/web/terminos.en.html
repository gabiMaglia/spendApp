Terms of use

spendApp

1 · What spendApp is

An app for recording and splitting expenses between people. It runs on your phone: there is no account on a server of ours holding your data, and we don't see what you enter.

2 · It is a record-keeping tool, not a payment method

spendApp does not move money. It doesn't process payments, transfer, charge, or act as an intermediary in any transaction. When you mark a debt as settled, you are recording that a payment happened outside the app; we don't verify it and can't. The numbers you see are the ones you entered. The accounts between you are yours.

3 · The data lives on the phones

  • If you lose your phone and didn't export a backup, the data is gone. We have no copy to restore it from. The app has backup export: use it.
  • What you shared with a group stays on that group's phones, even if you later delete your account. We can't delete it from there, and deleting it would break other people's balances.

4 · Your responsibility

  • Enter data you have the right to enter. If you add someone to a group, their expenses and their name will end up on the other members' phones.
  • Don't use the app for anything illegal.
  • Look after your phone: whoever has it unlocked has your data, because it's there and not behind a server.

5 · What we don't guarantee

The app is provided as is. In particular:

  • Syncing depends on the phones connecting. If someone doesn't open the app, their changes don't arrive. Pending packets expire after 30 days.
  • There can be temporary differences between two phones until they sync.
  • We don't guarantee the calculations are valid for any legal or accounting purpose. It's a tool for sorting out expenses among people who know each other, not a ledger.

6 · Account and deletion

You sign in with Google or Apple. You can delete your account from the app; what that deletion covers and what it doesn't is on the account deletion page and in the privacy policy.

7 · Price

Today the app is free and ad-free. If that changes, you'll be told inside the app before it happens, and what you already entered stays yours and exportable.

8 · Changes

If these terms change, the date on this page changes. A change affecting what leaves your phone is announced inside the app.
