Privacy policy

spendApp

The short version

spendApp runs on your phone. We have no database with your expenses, your name or your email. We can't see what you enter, and we don't want to.

The only thing that leaves your phone is encrypted packets addressed to the people in your groups, and not even we can open them: the key is on the phones, never on the server.

What data exists, and where

On your phone, and only there: your expenses, groups, payments and personal entries; your name, your photo and your preferences; your groups' cryptographic keys and your device identity. If you uninstall the app without exporting a backup, it's gone: we have no copy to restore it from.

On the phones of the people in your groups: when you share a group, its expenses —and your name and photo— are stored on each member's phone. Once data has arrived there, neither you nor we can delete it.

On our relay server (“the mailbox”): so two phones can sync without being together, the app leaves packets in a temporary mailbox. For each one we store a label derived from the group key, the encrypted content, an identifier of the sending device —not your name or email— and the arrival and expiry dates. Your email, name, photo and expenses are never stored in the clear, and packets delete themselves after 30 days, read or not.

One more thing, because it's true: whoever has or had a group's key can read its packets. An outsider can't, because without the key the label can't be guessed nor the content decrypted.

Currency rates

If you have expenses in more than one currency, the app requests the day's rate table from a public third-party service. That call sends no data of yours: it asks for the whole table, without saying who you are or what you spent. As with any internet request, that service sees your IP address. If you use a single currency, the call never happens.

Signing in

You can sign in with Google or Apple. From there we receive your provider identifier, your email and —if you share it— your name and photo. That data is stored on your phone. We don't send it to any server of ours and we don't use it to contact you: we have no user list.

Permissions

  • Camera: scan a contact's QR code and photograph a receipt.
  • Photos: pick your profile photo or a receipt.
  • Notifications: tell you a new expense arrived or someone asked to delete one. They are generated on your phone, not sent by a server.
  • Internet: leave and fetch packets in the mailbox.

The app does not request the microphone, Bluetooth or location.

What we don't do

No advertising and no advertising identifiers. No analytics: we don't measure which screens you see or how much you use the app. We don't sell or share data with anyone —there's no one to share it with: we don't have it—. No cross-app tracking.

Deleting your account

From Me → Delete account, everything belonging to that account is deleted from your phone, and the packets left by that phone are removed from the mailbox. The details, and what can't be deleted, are on the account deletion page.

Additional retention period: encrypted packets in the mailbox delete themselves 30 days after arriving, read or not.
