Privacy policy

HushSplit · updated September 29, 2026

The short version

HushSplit works on your phone. Your expenses, groups and contacts are not in any database of ours: they travel end-to-end encrypted between the phones in your group, through a mailbox that cannot open them.

On the server side we keep only what the mailbox needs to recognize your account: if you sign in with Google or Apple, your email and your account identifier. If you use the app as a guest, not even that.

What data exists, and where

On your phone: your expenses, groups, payments and personal entries; your name, photo and preferences; your groups' cryptographic keys and your device identity. If you uninstall the app without exporting a backup, it's gone: we have no copy to restore it from. The backup file includes your data and your group keys unencrypted: keep it private.

On the phones of the people in your groups: when you share a group, its expenses —and your name and photo— are stored on every member's phone. Once data has reached someone else's phone, neither you nor we can delete it from there.

At the sync mailbox (hosted on Supabase), each packet stores: a label derived from the group key, the encrypted content, an identifier of the sending device, a mark that lets that device delete it later, and its arrival and expiry dates. Packets delete themselves after 30 days. Anyone who has or had a group's key can read its packets; an outsider cannot, because without the key the label can't be guessed and the content can't be decrypted.

Your account at the mailbox:

  • If you sign in with Google or Apple: your email, your identifier with that provider and what the provider includes at sign-in (with Google, your name and photo address).
  • If you use the app as a guest: an anonymous account, with no email or name.
  • In both cases, your device's public key, so your groups can verify that what you write is yours, and usage counters (how many packets and bytes you send) to stop abuse. Counters delete themselves within a day at most.

This data is used only to make syncing work. We don't email you, we don't use it for advertising and we don't share it with anyone.

Third-party services

  • Supabase hosts the mailbox and the mailbox accounts.
  • Google and Apple, if you sign in with them. With Google, the app downloads your profile photo once from Google's servers.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, if you use the app as a guest: a bot check. Cloudflare sees your IP address and technical data from the app's built-in browser.
  • Exchange rates: if you have expenses in more than one currency, the app fetches the day's table from a public service (open.er-api.com). It sends no data about you; like any internet request, that service sees your IP. With a single currency, it never happens.

Permissions

  • Camera: scan a contact's QR code and take a photo of a receipt.
  • Photos: pick your profile photo, using the system picker. On Android it asks for no gallery permission.
  • Notifications: tell you about new expenses, comments, payments, restorations and invitations. They're generated on your phone, not sent by a server.
  • Internet: drop off and pick up packets at the mailbox.

The app does not ask for the microphone, Bluetooth or location.

What we don't do

No advertising and no advertising identifiers. No analytics: we don't measure which screens you see or how much you use the app. We don't sell or share data with anyone. No cross-app tracking.

Deleting your account

From Me → Delete account everything for that account is deleted from your phone; from the mailbox, the packets that phone left; and your mailbox account: email, identifier, public key and counters. If there's no internet at that moment, the server part is completed the next time you open the app online. If you no longer have the app, write to us from your account's email and we'll delete it. Details, and what can't be deleted, are on the account deletion page.
