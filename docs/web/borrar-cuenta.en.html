Delete your spendApp account

How to do it

  1. Open spendApp.
  2. Go to “Me”.
  3. Tap “Delete account” and confirm.

You don't need to write to us or wait for anyone: deletion happens right away, from your phone.

What is deleted

From your phone, everything belonging to that account:

  • expenses, groups, payments and personal entries;
  • your profile, your photo, your preferences and your keys;
  • the link between your Google and Apple accounts, if you had made one.

From the sync mailbox, the encrypted packets left there by that phone are removed right away.

What is NOT deleted, and why

The expenses that already reached the phones of the people in your groups. They are the other side of other people's debts: deleting them would break their balances. They will keep seeing them. What does disappear is your name and your photo: deleting your account notifies your groups, and you appear as “Deleted account”.

If you had open debts, deleting your account does not cancel them: the group still sees what was owed.

How long anything is kept

Encrypted packets left in the mailbox by an earlier install —for example, if you reinstalled the app— are deleted on their own after 30 days. That is the only retention period that exists, and it doesn't depend on anyone doing anything.

What we can't do for you

Your account doesn't exist on any server of ours. We don't have your email, your name or any way to know who you are: there is no row we could delete. That's why deletion happens in the app and not here.
